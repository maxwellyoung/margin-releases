# Margin releases

Signed and notarized builds of Margin for macOS, plus the update feed (`appcast.xml`) the app checks.

Download the newest `Margin.dmg` from [Releases](https://github.com/maxwellyoung/margin-releases/releases/latest), open it and drag Margin to Applications. Margin then keeps itself up to date: every update is signed, and the app installs one only if its signature matches.

Margin keeps your library on your Mac. See what can leave it in the app's Settings and privacy notes.
